The joint measure maps one uniform rank p to (Q_r(p), Q_s(p)). Its marginals
are the normalized angle laws. At interior ranks the quantile derivative
in field strength is minus the polar flux divided by the weight. Its mean
square equals parallelEnergy. Integrating the squared displacement along
the strength interval uses the analytic bound parallelEnergy <= 1/12.
The squared Wasserstein cost is the infimum in ENNReal of the angular
squared cost over all joint measures with the prescribed marginals.
The witness has integrable cost. Each periodic heat bath is projected
onto the polar angle about its own staple pole. A lift to a coupling
of SU(2) links and control of changes in pole direction remain open.
The heat-bath theorem identifies both poles through the normalized
Haar tilts. With all 18 incident normalized chords bounded by δ, the
angular cost is at most 27 β² δ². For δ > 0 and 0 <= β < sqrt(3)/9
this is strictly below δ². No quadrature enters these inequalities.
