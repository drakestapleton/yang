Both lattices are periodic. For coarse side l >= 2 and integer b >= 1
the site map is x -> b*x on the fine lattice of side b*l. Each retained
oriented edge is the forward product of b fine links. Every interior
gauge transformation cancels, including along paths ending across a seam.
A coarse spatial plaquette is a b-by-b fine loop. The four paths contain
4b distinct fine links and stay in the same fine time slice. The blocked
law is the pushforward of the normalized fine Wilson law. Pullback
preserves expectations and covariances of continuous functions.

For 0 <= beta < sqrt(3)/18 and alpha = 18 beta/sqrt(3), the connected
correlation of two blocked spatial plaquette traces on slices separated
by cyclic distance k is bounded in absolute value by
    (4 b pi^2 / (1 - alpha)) (alpha^b)^k.
A time translate by k gives that bound whenever 2k <= l. At beta = 12/125
and b = 2, alpha^b is exactly 15552/15625. Lean checks the rational identity.
WolframAlpha independently returned the same fraction.

With beta <= beta_max < sqrt(3)/18 and beta_max > 0, a signed sum of
spatial loop-pair covariances inherits the envelope multiplied by the
sum of the absolute values of its coefficients. The conditional spectral
corollary assumes a W0/W1/W2 theory. For each vacuum-orthogonal state the
signed sums must converge at all times k b R to its diagonal spectral
Laplace transform. For each fixed k, its separation and statewise uniform
coefficient bound need only hold for sufficiently large tori. The
resulting gap is log(1/alpha_max)/R, with R the fixed fine physical
spacing. Existence of such a spectral realization and control of a
weak-to-strong coupling flow remain open. The pushforward need not be
identified with a single-coupling coarse Wilson action for this result.
