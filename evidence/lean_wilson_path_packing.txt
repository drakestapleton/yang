Model and scope
---------------
proofs/WilsonReplicaPathPacking.lean imports the existing WilsonReplicaFreeShell.
For arbitrary compatible PinnedFrame P and mask m, FreeFacePath is an inductive
chain of actual Wilson Face l values, not an abstract graph of assumed split
faces. A cons step carries two members of P.freeFace p, which is the existing
faceEdges p minus P.edges. Its second link is literally the next step's first
link. The terminal empty path has one free endpoint. Incidence therefore uses
the existing periodic Wilson plaquette corners; pinned links cannot witness a
step. No path step assumes p belongs to P.freeBoundary m.

path_crosses_freeBoundary: different labels at the two endpoints force an
actual path face into P.freeBoundary m. Equal labels propagate by induction;
a changed label contradicts the existing freeUniform definition. The empty
path is ruled out by the opposite-label premise, not by a hidden nonemptiness
assumption.

path_packing_le_freeBoundary: given n such chains, opposite endpoint labels,
and pairwise disjoint face supports, choose one boundary witness per chain.
Disjointness makes that choice injective into P.freeBoundary m, proving
n <= (P.freeBoundary m).card. Paths may share links/endpoints and may revisit
their own faces; only different chains must have disjoint face supports.
The theorem does not assume that chains exhaust all lattice faces.

overlapping_paths_counterexample: a concrete side-two Wilson plaquette at
site zero with directions (0,1), the empty compatible frame, and the mask
selecting direction zero give a genuine one-step crossing. Repeat it
card(freeBoundary)+1 times. Every path and opposite-label premise holds,
but the count fails. Thus dropping inter-path face disjointness is genuinely
false, not merely beyond the tactics used here.

CONCRETE DATA LIMIT: this file does not construct the side-eight pinned tree,
the two temporal arcs, their face-disjoint packing, or the whole-slice labels.
Those are separate geometry certificates/data. In particular it does not
claim a Lean-certified side-eight numerical minimum or optimal flat cuts.
It proves the general certificate implication on actual Wilson incidence.
No configuration, Wilson action, exchange field, chain, stationarity,
continuum, ensemble decay rate or mass-gap assertion is proved here.
