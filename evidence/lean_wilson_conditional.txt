For every real beta and every positive torus side the Wilson joint law in
background/link coordinates is its background marginal composed with the
normalized link kernel. The kernel equals Mathlib’s regular conditional
law almost everywhere under that marginal. For sides at least two the
kernel has the previously proved six-staple Haar tilt. The angular
pushforward of Haar and the transport comparison for W2 remain open.

The subsequent angular-law proof identifies the Haar and Wilson angle densities.
