For a pole angle D the compensating shift sweeps unnormalized mass D around
each circle orbit. At intermediate time t its angular velocity relative to the
pole rotation is 1 - exp(-r x), where x is the scalar coordinate after the
mass-preserving shift. With Z(r) the Haar integral of exp(r x), symmetry gives
Z(-r)=Z(r), so the centered tilted mean square is 2(1-1/Z(r)).
Integration by parts against sin²(t) exp(r cos(t)) and the proved sine moment
bound give Z'(r) ≤ (r/4) Z(r). Hence Z(r) ≤ exp(r²/8), and the mean-square
circle speed is at most r²/4. Integration along the measure-preserving flow
and composition with the common-pole radial coupling give a complete full-link
map at equal strength and a geodesic coupling bound at different strengths.

For the periodic Wilson conditional laws the uniform row is
18 beta sqrt(1/12 + pi²/16). A single matrix bounds simultaneous link changes
and all powers acting on bounded initial profiles, including repeated
incidences on small periodic tori. W0/W1/W2 imply that the normalized vacuum
spectral measure is the Dirac measure at zero. A relativistic gap at fixed
physical shell spacing R additionally requires both an observable comparison
to the finite-volume path weights and convergence of those shell observables
to the diagonal Laplace transform of each vacuum-orthogonal state.
