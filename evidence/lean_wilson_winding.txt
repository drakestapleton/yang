At beta zero, one of the four distinct links of any plaquette can be
resampled from normalized SU(2) Haar. Holding the other three fixed
right-translates this link, so the entire plaquette also has Haar law.
This applies to every side l >= 2, including wrapped plaquettes.

Write X = Re Tr(H)/2 for plaquette holonomy H. The normalized trace of H^3
is 4 X^3 - 3 X by diagonal conjugacy. The Haar moments of X at degrees
one through four are 0, 1/4, 0, 1/8. Hence the two traces have covariance
-1/4. For normalized Haar on the circle the real parts of Fourier characters
1 and 3 have zero covariance. The abelian result uses Fourier orthogonality
at Haar and makes no claim about the interacting U(1) model at nonzero beta.

The SU(2) coupling derivative varies every plaquette simultaneously in the
four-dimensional Wilson action. If a face differs from the observed face,
one of its base links is outside the observed face. Haar averaging that link
kills its contribution. Differentiating the normalized measure and centering
the observable yield, for any continuous real f on the fundamental group,
the exact derivative at zero equal to the Haar integral of X f(H).

The primitive trace therefore has first derivative +1/4 and the triply wound
trace has first derivative -1/4. For every b > 0, the latter expectation
fails to be nondecreasing on [0,b]. A continuous increasing function of X
has nonnegative first derivative at zero, by X (f(X)-f(0)) >= 0. Extending
that conclusion to nonzero couplings or to blocked observables requires
new estimates. These results test a proposed monotonicity of loop observables.
The vacuum spectral gap and the asymptotic Wilson string tension are distinct
quantities, and their monotonicity is not decided by this test.
