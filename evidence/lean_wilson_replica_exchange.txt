Exact identity under the normalized Wilson law
Sample two independent configurations U,V. For a mask i let S_i exchange
the links in that mask between U and V. Write J_i = (log w - log w∘S_i)/2
for the joint Boltzmann weight w. Only faces split by the mask contribute
to J_i. If no plaquette is split by two masks and every mask flips
D=(F(U)-F(V))(G(U)-G(V)), then 2 Cov(F,G) = E[D product_i tanh J_i].
Product Haar invariance under link exchanges proves the identity at all
finite beta. Shells need no probabilistic independence.

On a fine periodic torus of side b*l, l >= 2, b >= 1, take F and G to be
products of normalized straight-blocked spatial plaquette traces on slices
s,u of the coarse torus. Exactly k=b*cyclicDistance(s,u) fine slab masks
separate them. Hence |Cov| <= 2 E[product_{j<k}|tanh J_j|].
The trace products can have arbitrary lengths and repeated plaquettes.

Pinned frames and the endpoint formula
A frame constructed from links it pins sends their product Haar prior to
identities on those links and independent Haar coordinates on the rest.
The proof conditions on the selected links and uses left and right Haar
invariance on the complementary coordinates. It then transports the
gauge-invariant Wilson weight to this pinned prior. Growing a rooted
forest admits either edge orientation. Both the signed identity and the
absolute slab bound hold in this field-dependent frame.

For spatial plaquettes on distinct, nonadjacent periodic time slices,
exchange the four links of each endpoint. No face crosses both masks.
Pinning three sides of each plaquette leaves one fluctuating side e.
In its own frame each replica has link matrix U_e and oriented staple
matrix S_U (a sum of six staples). The endpoint factor uses
  J_e = (beta/4) Re Tr[(U_e - V_e)(S_U - S_V)].
Cauchy-Schwarz bounds |J_e| by |beta|/4 times the product of the two
Frobenius differences. The geometric and energy identities are exact,
including the sign and the fundamental trace normalization. The concrete
4^4 test below builds the six-link forest used by the measurement.

Physical decay criterion
Let A_j=|tanh J_j|, W_j=product_{i<j} A_i, and M_j=E[W_j].
Then M_0=1 and M_{j+1}=M_j-E[W_j(1-A_j)]. The event A_j <= q_j pays
at least (1-q_j) E[W_j 1_{A_j<=q_j}] toward the next loss. If it pays
(1-exp(-m R)) M_j at every intervening fine shell, |Cov| <= 2 exp(-m R k).
Here R is the fine physical spacing. The more general loss theorem allows
unequal per-shell exponent budgets. A continuum use requires a common
positive m as spacing, volume, bare coupling and separation vary.

CPU experiment
Metropolis sweeps use symmetric quaternion proposals and the full periodic
Wilson staples. Both independent replicas are updated in each run.
Measurements use elementary spatial 0-1 plaquettes at t=0,2, with all
three spatial coordinates zero. Slab masks include every link based at
cyclic distance at most 0 or 1. Local masks contain the source plaquette
and then all links on faces meeting it. Endpoint masks contain just the
source or target plaquette. The rooted spanning tree and the pair of
three-edge trees are fixed before sampling. The per-field frame uses
parallel transport on the selected edges.

Each row records eight contiguous batch means and their standard error.
Hot and cold starts are separate Markov runs. Errors quantify variation
among these batches and omit possible thermalization bias. The diagnostic
uses binary64. Stable losses evaluate 2 exp(-2|J|)/(1+exp(-2|J|)) and
can still underflow. A displayed unit factor is rounded. None of the
sample means or errors enter a Lean proof.

The norm-test columns measure the event that the Frobenius upper bound
on |J| is <= atanh(0.9). The second event carries the first shell weight.
The TSV also reports the event tested against the actual |tanh J|, stable
losses, signed estimates, and E[|D| product A]/2 for each mask choice.
The compact U(1) control uses unit quaternions in the fixed real-i plane
and symmetric proposals in that same plane. Endpoint attenuation occurs
in that Abelian control as well. The numerical comparison at matched bare
beta does not match the physical scales of the two theories. The Lean
statements above concern SU(2).
All runs use separation two. The endpoint improvement supplies only two
factors whatever the separation. Interior slab factors saturate on these
tests. Establishing weighted overlap or signed cancellations at increasing
physical separations, and constructing the continuum spectral comparison,
remain open.

Relative color averaging
The joint Wilson law is invariant under global conjugation in the second
replica. Lean proves that one may average a signed shell estimator over
any nonempty finite family of such conjugations before taking its absolute
value. The endpoint theorem applies directly to the two pinned staples.
For SU(2), the CPU probe uses conjugation by the quaternion units 1,i,j,k.
These flip pairs of imaginary coordinates. For U(1), all internal color
orientations fix every link. The estimator checks the color action against
the frame and plaquette traces. Both absolute comparisons in the table
use exactly the same orientations and paired configurations.

Exact controls
The Wolfram kernel simplified the normalized two-flip sum with weights
exp(-2(i x+j y)) and signs (-1)^(i+j) to tanh(x)tanh(y) for real x,y.
For two identical random factors taking 1/4 and 3/4 with equal probability,
the exact product mean is 5/16 while the product of their means is 1/4.
