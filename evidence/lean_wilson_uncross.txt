freeBoundary_complement: globally complementing a mask preserves its actual free
boundary. Either endpoint orientation may therefore be aligned independently
before applying the remaining statements. Protected-link labels must be checked
on the resulting aligned inputs; arbitrary unaligned anchor labels are not an
assumption of the uncrossing theorem.

sorted_uniformity: if one input is uniform on the free links of a face, pointwise
Boolean meet and join equal a fixed permutation of the two original inputs on
that face. The permutation may differ by face. This includes empty free faces:
no free-link witness or nonemptiness hypothesis is used.

freeBoundary_uncross: disjoint input free boundaries imply disjoint output free
boundaries and exact equality of their unions. Input alignment is not required
for this geometric statement. A split may move from one mask to the other.
freeBoundary_uncross_card: using both disjointness statements and union equality,
the sum of the two actual freeBoundary cardinalities is exactly preserved.

uncross_nested_labels: the meet's true-link set is contained in the join's, and
both outputs preserve any prescribed label shared by the inputs on any finite
protected-link set. The prescribed label may vary with the link, so the set can
contain both source and target neighborhoods with opposite labels. It need not
be a whole slice or a face, and labels outside the set are unconstrained.

exists_nested_aligned_pair combines these properties: every aligned admissible
pair has a nested, aligned, boundary-disjoint representative of equal total
geometric cost. Thus restricting optimization of this cost and these protected
labels to nested pairs loses no candidate cost. A nested pair is itself an
aligned pair. This is an abstract implication, not a Lean construction or
numerical optimum certificate for the concrete side-eight pinned geometry.
The concrete optimizer's feasibility, capacities, exact minimum, masks and
measurements are separate evidence. No heap, action, exchange-field or measured
loss equivalence, continuum, equilibrium, attenuation or mass-gap claim is made.
