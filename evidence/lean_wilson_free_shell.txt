Statement and model-to-system link:
IsPinned requires both replica values to be identity on P.edges. freeFace is
faceEdges minus P.edges. freeUniform tests constant mask labels on those free
links, including the empty-support case. freeBoundary consists exactly of faces
that fail freeUniform. freeBoundary_disjoint identifies disjoint effective
boundaries with the pointwise disjunction required by the shell theorem.
exchange_eq_on_free proves labels on pins have no effect on an actual exchange.

free_field_stable proves exchange-field stability on pinned pairs whenever no
face is split by both masks on its free links. It uses the existing exact
exchangeField_eq_sum and trace_agrees, not an independent surrogate energy.
free_exchange extends a continuous integrand by erasing pinned coordinates on
arbitrary inputs. This erasure is continuous, commutes with exchange, always
lands on pinned pairs, and fixes pinned pairs. Thus oddness only on pinned pairs
suffices for the existing framed_exchange; pullback along P.pair removes the
extension in both integrals. No claim of full-space oddness of the original
integrand is used.

free_shell_identity proves the signed finite product under P.framedLaw beta,
with oddness only on pinned pairs and pairwise disjoint effective boundaries.
The masks are fixed functions of links, with no measured-field input.
free_plaquette_covariance takes arbitrary plaquettes p,q and any finite mask
family. Each mask must exchange every free link of exactly one endpoint and
leave the other's free links unchanged. It proves twice the ORIGINAL Wilson
covariance equals the expectation of the original replicaDifference times the
signed product of exchange fields evaluated on P.pair z. Gauge invariance of
the existing wilsonPlaquetteTrace supplies the projection bridge. No singleton
free-link assumption, spatial orientation, or stronger full-support separation
is imposed. free_anchor_labels supplies opposite endpoint labels from disjoint
free supports using the mask decide(e in freeFace p); reverse p,q for the other
endpoint. A candidate middle containing all free p links and excluding all free
q links meets the same endpoint condition. Free-boundary disjointness remains
an explicit, independently checkable geometry hypothesis.
